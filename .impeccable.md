## Design Context
### Users
Ordinary residents using phones, tablets and desktops to control their own home without learning Home Assistant. One home per installation.
### Brand Personality
Calm, clear, approachable. SMARTi brand, as requested by the creator: https://www.smarti.no/.
### Aesthetic Direction
Charcoal, warm orange, soft neutral surfaces; original SMARTi logo and locally bundled Bai Jamjuree. Quiet Scandinavian layout, both dark and light themes. Avoid technical dashboard clutter.
### Design Principles
- Make current state, stale data and pending actions unmistakable.
- Four primary destinations: Hjem, Rom, Enheter, Mer.
- Show only real capabilities; no decorative metrics or simulated live success.
- Large touch targets, keyboard focus, reduced motion and readable contrast.
- Keep secrets and privileged operations on the backend.
