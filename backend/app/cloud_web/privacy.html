SMARTi Home Privacy Policy

Who is responsible

SMARTi AS (Norwegian organisation number 933 234 649) is responsible for the personal data it processes for the SMARTi Home iPhone app, the SMARTi account portal and remote access. For privacy requests, email contact@smarti.dev or write to SMARTi AS, Sjekteveien 34B, 4624 Kristiansand, Norway.

What we process and why

  • Account and profile. We process your username, password hash, name, email, phone number, postal address and optional profile photo to run your account, verify your email address, help you recover access and handle support. The account form indicates which details are required to register.
  • Home access. We process account, home and paired phone identifiers, roles, sessions, and the name and optional photo you choose for your home. We use these details to manage access and give you the right controls. We use connection and sign-in information to protect accounts and keep the service available.
  • Devices and energy. Home Assistant on your hub processes device names, sensor readings, states, automations and history. If you use remote access, SMARTi relays the connection to the hub. When cloud energy history is enabled and your hub is connected to SMARTi, it uploads energy readings and electricity agreement settings to SMARTi. Cloud history is enabled by default in energy setup. You can turn it off in energy settings to stop future uploads.
  • Location. When you pair your iPhone and enable location sharing, the app sends your phone’s location, the accuracy and the time of the reading to Home Assistant on your hub. Home Assistant can use this data for presence and your automations. If you allow background location in iOS, updates may continue when the app is closed.
  • Notifications. If you enable notifications, your hub registers your phone’s push token and can store alerts in the notification inbox. Apple Push Notification service receives the token and alert content to deliver the notification.
  • Support. We process the messages and account or hub details needed to handle your request. If you approve temporary remote support access, we can use it to work on your request.
  • Marketing. We use your email address to send SMARTi newsletters or offers when you opt in. You can change your choice in your SMARTi account.

We process data to provide and support the service you request. Our legitimate interests include preventing misuse and keeping the service secure and available. Optional location sharing and marketing rely on your consent. The app uses the camera to scan a hub QR code and the local network to find a hub during setup. You can choose to take or select a profile photo. You can manage iPhone permissions in iOS Settings.

Who receives the data

People you give access to a home can see the data their role allows. We use providers for hosting, connectivity, email and notifications. Twilio SendGrid delivers account emails and receives recipient addresses and message contents. Apple receives push notification data when it delivers alerts. Opening a map sends requests for the visible area to OpenStreetMap, which receives connection information such as your IP address. Home Assistant integrations you enable may exchange data with their own providers under their own privacy policies.

Account email is sent through SendGrid’s global service and may be processed outside the European Economic Area. Twilio describes its safeguards for these transfers, including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where applicable and the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses when required. You can contact SMARTi for information about the safeguards for your data. Twilio Data Protection Addendum.

How long we keep data

We keep account and home access information while you use the service. Your sign-in persists until you sign out or access is revoked. Temporary remote support access expires after 30 minutes. Closing a support case leaves its conversation in your account. Account deletion removes your support cases and profile from the active portal database. We retain a record of account deletion for security and accountability. The relay keeps daily traffic totals per home for 90 days.

Home Assistant history and backups follow the settings on your hub. The cloud energy archive has no scheduled expiry. The service removes it when the hub confirms completion of a factory reset. Database copies made for recovery before portal updates may still contain data removed from the live portal.

Your choices and rights

In SMARTi Home, open Denne telefonen → Posisjonsdeling (This phone → Location sharing) to turn off location updates. You can also change your location and notification permissions in iOS Settings. To manage location or device history that Home Assistant already saved, use your hub’s settings. You can change your profile and marketing preferences in your SMARTi account.

To delete your SMARTi account, open Min konto → Slett kontoen din (My account → Delete your account). Remove your membership or unlink hubs you own before deleting the account. Contact us if you need help, want to withdraw consent or want to request deletion of other personal data held by SMARTi.

Depending on the law that applies, you can request access, correction, deletion, a copy of your data, or a restriction on processing. You can also object to certain uses or withdraw consent. In Norway, you can lodge a complaint with the Norwegian Data Protection Authority.
