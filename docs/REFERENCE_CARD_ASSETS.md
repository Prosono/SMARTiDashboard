# Assets for the November card reference

Created with the built-in `image_gen` tool for the card redesign. These are decorative illustrations, not live camera images or precise representations of a user's location, lunar phase or vacuum model. Readings, conditions and actions still come from the connected home. Originals remain in the local generated-images library. Project copies were resized and encoded as WebP with Pillow; transparency is preserved for the robot.

Saved project files:

- [Night weather](../frontend/public/home/weather/night.webp), 1280 × 853, 124,736 bytes.
- [Cloudy weather](../frontend/public/home/weather/cloudy.webp), 1280 × 853, 80,130 bytes.
- [Sunny weather](../frontend/public/home/weather/sunny.webp), 1280 × 853, 115,426 bytes.
- [Vacuum and dock](../frontend/public/home/equipment/vacuum-dock.webp), 640 × 427, transparent.

The user-supplied dashboard image guided the composition and materials. It was not embedded as an interface image. All controls and text are native interface elements.

## Final prompts

Night:

> Use case: photorealistic landscape photography. Asset type: background image for a smart home weather card. Wide landscape composition, a Norwegian lake seen from above the shoreline on a clear late autumn night. Deep navy blue sky, fine scattered wispy clouds, a small realistic bright full moon in the upper right quadrant. Silhouettes of pine trees along the lower edge, distant low mountains and a tiny line of warm amber village lights reflecting across calm water. The sky fills the upper two thirds, dark clean space on the left for white UI text to be placed later. Rich cinematic but natural night photography, detailed cloud texture and realistic exposure. This is only the scenic image, no UI, no text, no labels, no frame, no logos. Horizontal 3:2 image.

Cloudy:

> Use case: photorealistic landscape photography. Asset type: background image for a smart home weather card. Wide horizontal composition of a Norwegian lake and distant low mountains on a cloudy late autumn day, with a dramatic but quiet blue grey overcast sky filling the upper two thirds. A few silhouetted pine trees at lower right and a tiny distant village across calm reflective water. No sun, no moon, no snow, no rain drops on the lens. Natural soft blue slate and charcoal tones, subtle light behind the clouds, realistic landscape photography. Keep left half quiet for overlaid white UI text added later. This is only the scenic image, no UI, no text, no frame, no logos. Horizontal 3:2 image.

Sunny:

> Use case: photorealistic landscape photography. Asset type: background image for a smart home weather card. Wide horizontal view of a Norwegian lake in clear autumn daylight, distant low forested mountains and a few pine trees silhouetted at lower right. Clear rich blue sky fills the upper two thirds, just a few high wisps of cloud, a small naturally bright sun toward the upper right with restrained photographic bloom. Reflections across calm blue water, a tiny distant lakeside village. Realistic refined landscape photography, deep clean blue tones, no lens flare circles. Keep the left half quiet for overlaid white UI text added later. This is only the scenic image, no UI, no text, no frame, no logos. Horizontal 3:2 image.

Vacuum and dock (transparent background):

> Use case: photorealistic product cutout. Asset type: smart home dashboard robot vacuum illustration, displayed about 170 pixels wide on a mint green card. A premium white round robot vacuum with subtle dark grey bumper trim, a small white lidar turret on top, realistic clean materials. Next to its compact charcoal black rectangular charging dock, the dock sits just behind the robot on the right. Front three-quarter view from slightly above, robot large in foreground, dock modestly taller behind it. Soft studio lighting, subtle short contact shadows, crisp photorealistic product rendering, no floor or room. Isolated on genuinely transparent background, no logos, no branding, no text, no labels, no frame. Tight horizontal composition, generous transparent edge around the product.
